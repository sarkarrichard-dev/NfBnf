<objective>
ORD-03: the Dhan websocket reconnecting in the middle of a busy scan cycle must not silently
drop tick data.

Correction to RESEARCH.md Critical Finding 3 (re-read index_ai/tick_feed.py:181-263 on
2026-09-30): the tick buffer is created once at line 214, OUTSIDE the reconnect loop, so on a
hard ConnectionClosed the buffered ticks are not discarded immediately — they are carried into
the next connection and written at its first flush. The real losses are narrower but real:
(1) ticks sit unwritten in memory for the whole outage and back-off (up to 60 s between
attempts), (2) if the server is stopped while the feed is down, the loop breaks at lines
259-260 without flushing and those ticks are lost, and (3) a task cancellation on shutdown
skips the flush too. Separately, (4) a server "disconnect" packet only breaks the inner for-loop
(line 242), so the feed keeps waiting on a dead stream until the 90-second silence timer, and
(5) exceptions from on_tick — the stop-trigger handler — are swallowed with no trace
(lines 246-250). The stop trigger itself receives ticks at receive time, before the buffer, so
(1)-(3) affect the tick log, and (4)-(5) affect stop triggering. All five are fixed here.

What "recovers missed ticks" can mean (flagged below): ticks the exchange sent while the socket
was down cannot be fetched again from Dhan's live feed. Recovery here is: nothing received is
lost, the feed resubscribes and resumes at once, and the 20-second price check covers the gap
(plan 02-07 proves and documents that).

Pluralization note (D-01): Delta has no websocket in this codebase (crypto polls REST every 60 s),
so ORD-03 is Dhan-only by nature.
</objective>
