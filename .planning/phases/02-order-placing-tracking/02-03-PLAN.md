<objective>
Dhan's half of ORD-01, part 1 (after crypto's tracer, D-02): stop the India order path from
creating a duplicate or an untracked order when Dhan's reply goes missing.

Findings this plan acts on (verified 2026-09-30, beyond RESEARCH.md):
- index_ai/dhan.py:121-135 retries ANY request up to 4 times on httpx.HTTPError — including
  POST /orders. A read timeout after Dhan received the order sends a second real order. Delta's
  client already refuses this (crypto/delta/client.py:203-212); Dhan's does not. This is the
  single biggest duplicate-order risk found in the phase.
- index_ai/executor.py:244-253 turns any exception from place_live_entry_orders into
  LIVE_FAILED with no journal row — even when orders were already accepted and the failure
  happened while waiting for the fill (get_order during a disconnect). Those orders are then
  on Dhan with nothing tracking them.
- index_ai/dhan_orders.py place_live_exit_orders builds a random correlation id on every
  call, so a retry after a partly-failed exit re-sends legs that already went through; for a
  single-leg buy, a retried SELL after a lost reply can leave a naked short.
- Dhan cancel contract: DELETE {api_base_url}/orders/{order-id}, where api_base_url already
  ends in /v2 (reference/openalgo/broker/dhan/api/order_api.py:374-392). This resolves
  RESEARCH.md Assumption A2 — it is now cited, not assumed. The cancel itself is used by
  plan 02-04; this plan adds the method and the DELETE plumbing it needs.

Symmetric with plan 02-01 (D-01): Delta settles by client_order_id, Dhan by correlationId.
</objective>
