<objective>
Tracer slice for Phase 1: prove, end to end on real recorded data, that the live
option-buy lane's own entry function can be replayed against the real option chain
recorded since 2026-09-23, with ONE tightened gate switchable on and off — before any
other lever is added or anything reaches the paper lane.

The one gate is the ML model's own flagged insight (D-03): stop buying against the CPR
day direction and on a sideways CPR day. It is added buy-lane-only behind a new switch
that defaults off, because the global CPR width thresholds also drive the sell lane
(which is net-positive on NIFTY and therefore frozen under the confidence ladder) and the
exit logic (Phase 3). The replay is a new pair of Strategy Lab candidates (baseline =
today's settings, tuned = today's settings plus the gate), run on NIFTY first (D-02).

Purpose: D-08/D-09 require a real-chain sanity check before any paper change; no tool
today replays evaluate_buy_signal on real chain data (the old options backtest is a
Black-Scholes proxy of a different, retired strategy).
Output: switch + gate, replay adapter + two on-demand lab candidates + CLI, tests, and
the first real NIFTY baseline-vs-tuned numbers in the SUMMARY.
</objective>
