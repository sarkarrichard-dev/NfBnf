<objective>

all three indices.

D-01 asks to finish and tune the OI-wall support/resistance + fake-breakout work. Two
concrete gaps remain:
- Breakout patterns (breakout_resistance / breakdown_support) measure the 20-bar candle
  range and ignore the OI walls entirely, so the lane can buy a call a few points under the
  biggest call-OI strike — the classic fake breakout. New buy-lane gate (switch off by
  default): no buy unless there is at least one buy-trail distance of room to the opposing
  OI wall. The 1:1 trail cannot lock any profit before the index moves one trail distance
  (NIFTY 25 / BANKNIFTY 55 / SENSEX 80 points, each about 0.1% of the index), so a wall
  closer than that caps the trade at a loss or a scratch.
- Fake-breakout confirmation: ENTRY_CONFIRMATION_BARS 2 -> 3 in the tuned bundle, the same
  lever raised 1 -> 2 on 2026-09-16. On the live path this key feeds only the buy lane's
  detect_breakout.

The tuned bundle is then replayed next to today's settings on every recorded session for
NIFTY, BANKNIFTY and SENSEX (D-02: NIFTY judged first, the other two checked on the same
change), and the dashboard gains the rows Richard needs to confirm the switch-on later.

Output: second gate + switch, full LIVE_BUY_TUNED, dashboard rows, guide rows, and the
three-index baseline-vs-tuned table with a plain-English read in the SUMMARY.
